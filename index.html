About

I like analysis that someone acts on.

My work usually starts with a messy question from a product, marketing or leadership team and ends with a recommendation they can take into a roadmap meeting. At Wise that meant breaking an underperforming Philippines test into a funnel, finding that the variant had removed the currency calculator users relied on, and getting it put back.

I care about comparing like with like. When I picked routes for a CRM promotion, every customer got the same observation window before I compared route volumes against the wider market.

I've also built the other side: ML models at Bank Jago, an LLM feature on a knowledge graph at Gotrade, and data models in dbt that analysts across APAC now query. Outside work I run a self-directed equity portfolio and build research tools on GitHub.

Wise · Experimentation

Why the Philippines variant lost users

+7%projected conversion

ProblemA localisation test in the Philippines was underperforming and nobody knew which step was to blame.

MethodAdapted the GB pipeline to Philippine conventions in Snowflake, set a consistent 30-day action window per signup, and rebuilt the results as a step-by-step funnel.

ResultThe variant had removed the currency calculator, a high-converting first step. Bringing it back went onto the product roadmap.

Wise · Marketing

Picking routes for a CRM promotion

+5%projected LTV

ProblemMarketing wanted to know which APAC routes a transfer promotion should target.

MethodCompared route volumes against the market, giving every customer the same window to act, to separate routes with room to grow from routes declining month on month.

Result6 routes approved. If it works, it expands to 5 more.
